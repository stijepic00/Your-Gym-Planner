Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026.

GymLeader processes information needed for the application and user account: name or nickname, gender, age, height, weight, goal, training frequency, training location, experience, target muscle groups, exercises you want or avoid, allergies and foods you must avoid, email address, sign-in information, routines, workout history, weights, repetitions, duration, notes, body weight and circumference measurements, language, settings and technical data needed for security and synchronisation.

We use data to create and maintain accounts, provide sign-in and email verification, save routines and workouts, synchronise devices, secure the system and provide support. We do not sell data.

We use reliable technical providers, including Firebase, Vercel and Brevo, only as needed for their functions. If you activate payments in the future, Lemon Squeezy will process billing data under its own policies.

You may request access to, correction, deletion or export of your data where applicable by law. Send requests to gymleaderapp@gmail.com.

We retain data for as long as needed to operate your account, maintain system security or meet legal obligations. We use reasonable technical safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security or uninterrupted operation.

We may update this policy. Where a change requires renewed acceptance, we will display a new version before you continue using the application.

Language

The public site remembers only your language preference in browser storage and, on HTTPS GymLeader domains, in the gymleader-language cookie shared with the app. It contains only a language code, lasts up to one year and transfers your latest choice between the site and app. This site adds no analytics tracking.

For questions about these documents, email gymleaderapp@gmail.com.
