Contact us↗
What is GymLeader and who is it for?

GymLeader is a web app for organising routines, logging workouts and tracking progress. It is for people who want to manage their own training and nutrition records.

Do I need a new plan every week?

No. One plan is one saved workout that you can repeat whenever you want. For example, ‘Leg day’ stays in your routines until you change or remove it.

What if I don’t have a routine?

You can use the app’s plan suggestion generator. Review and adjust the suggestion before saving. The app asks for additional information if the generator needs it.

Do I need to download an app from a store?

Use GymLeader in a browser on your phone or computer. On supported devices you can add a shortcut to your home screen. To start, open gymleader.app.

What can I track during training?

You can log exercises, sets, weights, reps and notes, then review completed workouts, personal records and progress charts. Body tracking lets you optionally record weight and circumferences.

How accurate is the nutrition information?

Calories, macros and prices in Meal Planner are catalogue estimates. Check portions, ingredients and labels, especially if you have allergies. GymLeader does not replace a doctor or dietitian.

How do I sign in?

At gymleader.app you can create an account by email and confirm it with a verification code, or use the available Google sign-in. Access an existing account using your chosen sign-in method.
